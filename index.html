02 / Selected Work — 04

01 · Flagship In development · Private · Case study

roomstudio

"Every home contains a version of itself its owner has never seen."

A spatial-intelligence platform for the home. Scan your space with an iPhone Pro and roomstudio reconstructs both the room and its contents — measured walls, floor, doors and windows with inferred surface materials, and every object placed at its real metric position.

Then it talks about it. A conversational layer answers questions about your actual room, grounded strictly in measured scene facts — the raw geometry never reaches the model. Redesigning the space from that conversation is the next stage.

The backend is deployed and has processed real captured rooms end to end. The web surface stays private pending pre-launch hardening.

Embedding space · pgvector

gitsearch

Semantic search for GitHub. Ask in plain English; three retrieval lanes — full-text, dense vectors, and fuzzy name matching — are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then blended with saturating popularity and floored recency. Every result explains its own ranking, and each carries a usage guide an agent writes after reading the repository's actual files.

Ranking changes are gated on a 200-query evaluation harness with 9,492 graded judgements — which has vetoed two finished changes for missing their pre-declared threshold.
